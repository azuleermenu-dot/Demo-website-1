01 / The idea

A flexible identity
for real clients.

This demo intentionally uses a made-up restaurant concept. There is no claim that PLATE / 01 is a real restaurant, location, menu, or operating business.

The useful part is the design system: content, menu cards, booking flow, colors, typography, spacing, borders and motion can all change without rebuilding the whole page.
